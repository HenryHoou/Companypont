Semantic parts, random crops, single scans, sparse or noisy points, and monocular depth—all handled without completion-specific training.

The challenge

Generative priors need a stable trajectory.

Pretrained 3D generative models offer powerful shape priors, but unconstrained latent noise and uniform sampling schedules expand the solution space excessively. The result is unstable completion, geometric artifacts, and poor fidelity to observations.

CompAnyPoint instead guides exploration from observed structures, preserves reliable geometry through soft replacement, and concentrates computation on the stages where geometry actually forms.

Method

Control space. Control time.

Two coupled mechanisms stabilize a frozen pretrained generator throughout the entire sampling process.

Spatial control

Latent-Space Geo-Prompt

Controls where new geometry emerges by coupling a noisy exploration branch with a clean fidelity branch.

  • Spatial growth expands from observed latent supports.
  • Best-view control optionally directs monocular completion.
  • Soft replacement maintains observation fidelity and continuity.
Dynamic control

Dynamics Reallocation Sampling

Controls how sampling evolves by matching computation and projection strength to geometric dynamics.

  • Timestep reallocation focuses on middle-stage structure formation.
  • Adaptive projection shifts from loose exploration to tight consolidation.
  • Fewer evaluations improve quality and efficiency together.
