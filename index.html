CompAnyPoint

CompAnyPoint stabilizes the sampling process of a pretrained 3D generative model through two complementary controls:

(a) Latent-Space Geo-Prompt (LSG) guides stochastic exploration from observation-derived spatial supports. Spatial growth encourages geometry to expand from reliable observations, optional best-view control directs monocular completion, and soft replacement preserves observed geometry while maintaining structural continuity.

(b) Dynamics Reallocation Sampling (DRS) adapts the sampling process to non-uniform geometric evolution. Generator-side timestep reallocation concentrates evaluations on geometry-forming stages, while decoder-side adaptive projection shifts from permissive exploration to strict occupancy consolidation.
